# Asset credits

## Team-created game assets

The following project-specific assets were created by the Dino Runner university team for this game:

- Character sprites: `stegosaurus_walking.png`, `stegosaurus_jumping.png`, `stegosaurus_ducked.png`, `stegosaurus_with_jump.png`
- Obstacles and effects: `Kaktus.png`, `Stein.png`, `Feuer.png`, `fireball.png`, `arrow.png`
- Environment: `cloudy_sky.png`
- Collision data: `Kaktus_outline.txt`, `Stein_outline.txt`, `Feuer_outline.txt`, `stegosaurus_jumping_outline.txt`
- Collision sound: `drop.mp3`

These files are included to document and run the educational team project. No standalone reuse permission is granted.

## Framework assets

`libgdx.png` and the files under `assets/ui/` belong to the libGDX project/template rather than the student team. They remain subject to their upstream terms and are not claimed as original team artwork.
